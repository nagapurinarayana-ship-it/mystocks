MY STOCKS · METHODOLOGY

Research methodology & risk policy

A transparent explanation of what the dashboard measures, where data comes from and what it does not claim.

Two separate research layers

Long-term watchlist research evaluates the business thesis, balance-sheet and governance risks. The pre-market card is a separate short-horizon rules-based signal generated from completed daily candles. A short-term signal never overrides a filing-based thesis breaker.

SOURCE HIERARCHY

Material company facts

  1. NSE/BSE regulatory filings and audited financial statements
  2. Company investor-relations disclosures
  3. Credit-rating agency rationales
  4. Market-data providers as secondary cross-checks
  5. News aggregators for discovery only
DATA QUALITY

Automatic checks

Displayed market prices are reconciled against trading history when quote metadata is inconsistent. Affected valuation fields are suppressed rather than shown as confidently correct. Missing data is not silently treated as a positive signal.

PRE-MARKET SIGNAL

Rules before a pick is published

The model uses completed daily candles only, a Nifty trend gate, liquidity/history filters, historical sample requirements, confidence bounds and expected-value filters. If no stock qualifies, the correct output is NO TRADE. Reference targets and stops are research parameters, not guaranteed outcomes.

RISK

Long-term thesis breakers

  • Promoter pledge, major unexplained promoter exit or repeated dilution.
  • Qualified/adverse/disclaimer audit opinion or going-concern concern.
  • Persistent operating-cash-flow deterioration while accounting profit remains positive.
  • Material default, sharp credit deterioration or debt escalation without executable growth.
  • Core-business change that invalidates the original investment thesis.

Important limitation

This is a personal research dashboard, not investment advice or a promise of returns. Market data can be delayed or incomplete, backtests can fail out of sample and micro-cap equities can lose most or all of their value. Verify material facts in official filings and make independent investment decisions.

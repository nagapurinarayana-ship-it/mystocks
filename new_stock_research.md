# New-stock research policy

Stocks with less than five years of history are **not excluded**. They are separated into confidence tiers so a new listing cannot look equally reliable to a mature stock simply because its short history contains a few winners.

| History | Treatment |
|---|---|
| 5+ years | Full historical confidence |
| 3–5 years | Reduced confidence; require robust sample count |
| 1–3 years | Exploratory; lower ranking weight |
| 6–12 months | Early-signal research only; never treated as equivalent to 5Y evidence |
| <6 months | Insufficient for this strategy |

For newer stocks, MyStocks should additionally compare:

- volatility and ATR against sector peers
- liquidity and turnover
- sector/index-relative momentum
- comparable historical patterns in older peer stocks
- IPO/listing-age effects
- corporate actions and trading suspensions
- sample size and confidence interval

A new stock can enter the daily shortlist only when its current setup has enough direct evidence **or** strong peer/sector evidence. Its confidence label must remain visible.

Never backfill pre-listing prices. Never use future index membership to construct a historical universe.
